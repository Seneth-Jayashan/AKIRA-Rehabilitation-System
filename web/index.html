Scientific Foundations

Clinical Balance, Sway Geometry & Smoothness

Mathematical principles behind our Center of Pressure (CoP) convex hull stability index, RMS Jerk derivatives, and edge mobile deployment.

1. Center of Pressure (CoP) Convex Hull Sway Area

The Center of Pressure (CoP) represents the focal point of the body's downward force vector on the ground plane. Using computational geometry, our pipeline computes the Convex Hull (the minimal bounding polygon enclosing all (cop_x, cop_y) coordinates over a 2.0s window) to derive the mathematical Sway Area in cm².

Finding: Healthy stance exhibits a tight focal cluster (Sway Area < 2.5 cm², Stability > 90%). Post-ORIF ligament laxity produces wide multi-directional sway loops (Stability < 35%).

2. Kinematic Smoothness: RMS Jerk & SPARC

Knowing what exercise a patient is doing is only half the diagnosis; clinicians must measure movement quality. We calculate RMS Jerk (the 3rd derivative of position, m/s³) and SPARC (Spectral Arc Length) over the Fourier spectrum to detect spastic muscle tremors and hesitation during ankle flexion.

Metric Verification: Fluid coordinated gait yields RMS Jerk of 2.23 m/s³, whereas post-operative compensatory spasms spike above 18.2 m/s³.

3. Explainable AI: SHAP Global Feature Attributions

To avoid black-box clinical decision making, we extracted SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) across all exercise classes. The analysis confirms that vertical acceleration entropy (az_freq_entropy) and gyroscopic variance (gx_var) are the primary differentiators between rhythmic walking and static balance.

4. Edge Deployment & Arduino Wearable Setup

For clinical and at-home adoption, the models cannot rely on cloud servers. We optimized and serialized the top models into ONNX format for native execution inside the AKIRA Android app using ONNX Runtime Mobile.

  • rehab_xgb.onnx (564 KB): Ultra-lightweight gradient boosting tree for battery-efficient mobile execution.
  • rehab_rf.onnx (64.5 MB): High-capacity ensemble for clinical workstation evaluation.
  • Hardware Sensor Stream (Phase 8): Arduino Nano 33 BLE / MPU6050 sampling at 100Hz synchronously over BLE, matching the PHYTMO calibration frame.
