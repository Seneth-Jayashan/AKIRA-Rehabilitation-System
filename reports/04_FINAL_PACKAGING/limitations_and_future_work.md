# Final Limitations and Future Work

- **Healthy public dataset**: Model is trained exclusively on non-injured subjects.
- **No post-ORIF patients**: Cannot assert recovery correlation.
- **No longitudinal clinical recovery data**: Time-series progression over weeks is untested.
- **No clinical outcome labels**: PRWE/DASH scores absent.
- **No clinical effectiveness validation**: Strict boundary between kinematics math and therapy.
- **Limited number of subjects**: 15 total subjects, 2 isolated to test set.
- **Moderate exercise classification**: Accuracy implies sequence identity needs larger contexts.
- **Android inference latency**: NOT YET PHYSICALLY BENCHMARKED.
- **Next Experiments**: Trial deployment on an actual clinical ORIF cohort to capture true pathological drift.
